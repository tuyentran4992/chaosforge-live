01

Visual principles

THREE RULES

Disciplined neon

Glow signals borders, status and data; dark surfaces keep the focus clear.

Color is a signal

Violet is Twitch, emerald is Codex, amber is hype and time, and red is reserved for safety.

Dense, ordered HUD

Monospace presents machine data; Space Grotesk carries headings and conversation.

Clipped corners

Cut corners suggest hardware and controls while distinguishing the product from rounded cards.

Visual anchor

Each view has one focus: the live poll, judge prompt or panic control.

07

Motion and accessibility

CLEAR AND INCLUSIVE

Motion rhythm

Enter in 180ms and exit in 140ms using the shared easing curve; poll updates every 250ms.

Reduced motion

Reduce particles and transitions when requested; state must remain clear from text and color.

Keyboard

Space clears, WASD or arrows move the ship, and controls retain a visible focus indicator.

Language and content

Interface text is English. Chat ideas can be multilingual and are shown as submitted.
